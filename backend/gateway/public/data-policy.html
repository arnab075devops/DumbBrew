Data Storage Policy

Version {{ termsVersion }} · Written to align with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)

1. What we collect and why

When you create a DumbBrew account, we collect the following personal data ("Personal Data" as defined under the DPDP Act) directly from you, for the stated purpose only:

DataPurposeWhere it's stored
Email addressAccount identification, communicationAuthentik (identity provider) and our database (Supabase)
UsernameAccount identification, display nameAuthentik and our database
Phone numberAccount identification, order/delivery contactAuthentik (as an attribute) and our database
PasswordAccount securityAuthentik only, stored as a salted hash — never seen or stored by our own database
Consent record (timestamp + policy version)Proof of consent to these terms, as required under the DPDP ActOur database (Supabase)

We do not collect Personal Data from you for any purpose beyond operating your account and the service, and we do not sell your Personal Data.

2. Legal basis for processing

We process your Personal Data on the basis of your explicit, informed consent, given by checking the consent box on the registration page and accepting this policy. You may withdraw consent at any time by requesting account deletion (Section 6), though this will mean you can no longer use features that require an account.

3. Where your data lives

Your account credentials (password) are held only by our self-hosted identity provider, Authentik. Your profile data (email, username, phone number, consent record) is held in our database, Supabase. Product/menu images are served from Cloudflare R2 and contain no personal data. Each of these is a data processor acting on our instructions; none of them independently uses your data for their own purposes.

4. Cross-border data transfer

The DPDP Act permits transfer of personal data outside India except to countries specifically restricted by the Central Government — it does not, on its own, require your data to be stored only within India. Depending on the hosting region chosen for our database provider, your data may be processed outside India; we do not transfer data to any country restricted under the DPDP Act. The specific hosting region in use will be named here once finalised.

5. How we protect your data

  • Every customer's data is isolated at the database level: row-level security policies ensure a signed-in customer can only read or modify their own account row, never another customer's.
  • Passwords are never stored by DumbBrew's own systems — only by Authentik, hashed.
  • All data in transit is sent over HTTPS.
  • Access to raw production data is limited to what is operationally necessary.

6. Your rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and the processing activities carried out.
  • Correct inaccurate or outdated personal data.
  • Erase your personal data once it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected, or to withdraw consent entirely (this closes your account).
  • Grievance redressal — raise a complaint about how your data is handled, and have it addressed in a reasonable timeframe.
  • Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Grievance Officer below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe as required under the Act.

7. Data retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, we will delete your profile data and consent record, and request deletion of your Authentik identity, within a reasonable timeframe, except where retention is required to comply with a legal obligation.

8. Children's data

DumbBrew's account features are not directed at, and are not knowingly offered to, individuals below the age required for consent under the DPDP Act. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected data from a child without verifiable parental consent, we will delete it.
